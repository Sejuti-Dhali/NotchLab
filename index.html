Model and limitations

H(s) = (s² + ω₀²)/(s² + 4ω₀s + ω₀²), where ω₀ = 1/(RC). The balanced passive Twin-T is followed by an ideal unity-gain buffer. The model assumes exact component ratios, ideal source and no loading. It does not model 741 bandwidth, noise, tolerance or saturation. The synthetic signal is an educational demonstration; no clinical validation or quantitative comparison against Multisim has been performed.
